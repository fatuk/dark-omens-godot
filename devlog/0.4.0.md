## 🇬🇧 English version

# 0.4.0 — Every Clue Remembers

A quieter update than the last, but a deeper one. This release is about **evidence** — the fragments an investigator drags out of the dark and carries in their hand — and about the small, physical feel of taking an action at all. The Tunguska table just got more tactile, and a good deal more literate.

## 🔍 Clues that remember

Until now a clue was a number. Now it's a **thing you found**.

Every location on the board can carry a clue written for the **Ancient One you're facing** — a title, a description, a face — authored in the content workshop alongside the doom clock and the Mythos deck. When you **research** a location, you don't just tick a counter: you take *that location's* clue, and it goes into your hand as a card you can read. Gain a clue by other means and one is drawn from the **remaining, unplaced** clues instead.

Clues are **unique for the game**: each belongs to exactly one place at a time — the available pool, the board, or a hand. Spend one, lose one, discard one, and it returns to the pool to be found again. It's the Eldritch-Horror rhythm of investigation, told in the Ancient's own words. Locations without written lore still hand you the place's name, so nothing you hold is ever anonymous.

## 🎛️ A new action panel

The bottom bar has been rebuilt from the frame up. Every action — **Rest, Focus, Resource, Pass** — is now a hand-painted medallion with a stretchable name plaque underneath, so every translation sits properly no matter its length. Token counts float **above** their buttons; your tickets stack vertically like a dealt hand; the clue tally sits to the side, always visible (even at ×0). The Pass hourglass turns a full 360° with a motion-blurred sweep before the turn actually passes.

## 🔔 Actions you can feel

Taking a resource should feel like *taking* something. Now, when you Rest, Focus, or claim a Resource, the token **grows toward the camera and dissolves** — and the game holds until the flourish finishes. Each of those actions has its own sound, too: a dedicated cue for Focus, Resource and Pass, and **five different healing tones** for Rest, picked at random so a long game never repeats itself.

## 💬 Messages, now as cards

The map-message composer from 0.3.0 got a face-lift: each message is drawn on an **ornate card** with a slow, living swirl of smoke in its art window — a different drift for every card. New **question** phrasings ("Where…?", "What…?", "Who…?") join the vocabulary, so you can leave a puzzle on the board, not just a warning.

## 🕯️ Polish & fixes

- changing UI text now shimmers through a **rune scramble** as it settles — round numbers, phases, investigator names, section titles;
- the **doom track** finally scales to the Ancient's own doom clock instead of a fixed length, so a near-full track means what it says;
- scrolling with a fan open no longer zooms the map;
- investigators keep their **starting clues and tickets** at setup;
- a colour-palette pass pulled scattered hard-coded colours into a single design-token theme;
- and a long, stubborn hunt finally cornered the case of the too-dark Pass hourglass (it was a shader multiplying the texture by itself — a story for another day).

**Thank you for playing.** The dark is keeping notes on you now — so keep better ones. See you in Tunguska.
